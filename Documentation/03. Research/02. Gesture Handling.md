## Research

### Drakheie
Drakheir is a roguelike adventure game, which was chosen for research as it is the most clear example of using hand gestures to cast elemental spells.

---

#### Gestures
Drakheir, has 3 types of spell and thus gestures, normal spells, combinations and finishers.  

Normal spells require you to make a hand-gesture with your palm facing towards you and then that spell is active in your hand, but pointing your hand at the enemy and flicking your wrist back you choose the selected spell. There are exceptions to this however. Elemental Weapons require both hands to trigger and might have different hand-movements while their active. And the shield activates with you making a fist and pushing it outward, then you are able to ‘throw’ it but making a motion as if throwing a frisbee, or a motion in the same vein as captain America would do when throwing his shield.  

Combinations require you to have two spells active and then holding both hands in front of you, acting as if you are holding a ball. Then a combination spell will charge up and when it is done charging you can push both your hands outward to cast the combination spell. Of course, you need to have it both elemental spells AND the combination first.  

Lastly are finishers, when attacking an enemy you can charge up a finisher on them, eventually, you hold out your hand and with your palm pointing towards yourself you can touch your thumb and middle finger together, a green orb will appear and if you then make a snapping motion with your fingers, time freezes and a mini-game starts. This requires you to do a sequence of 6 double handed hand gestures to cast a special spell with the element that you attacked the enemy with earlier. These hand gestures can include making a fist and holding a flat hand against it, making a rhombus with your hands while both of them are making a pointing gesture or making two fists and holding them out in front of you with your palms touching.

--- 

#### Movement
Drakheir has 3 movement systems, one of which can be active at a time, this can be switched at any point in the menu. These are: teleport, grab and hold, and tilt.

Teleport requires you to extend your arm, palm pointing downward and point with your thumb extended. A marker will then appear on the ground and you can touch thumb and pointer finger to teleport to the required destination.

Grab and hold also requires you to extend your arm, but now you can ‘grab’ and pull your arm backward to propel yourself forward.

Tilt is the most ‘unique’ movement mechanic in Drakheir. When this is active you clap your hands, causing a white circle to expend outward, indicating that you did it correctly. At that point, you can tilt or move your head in the direction you want to go in. Basically, when you clap it saves the current position of the headset as the central position and then your entire body effectively becomes a joystick. If you ever want to stop and look at something or try to dodge an attack by moving your head out of the way, you need to clap your hands again otherwise you would start moving again when your headset moves.

--- 

#### Tutorials
Whenever you unlock a new skill in Drakheir, you are put in a scenario in which you see a video which shows how to cast the spell and then you see a hologram of how you are supposed to move your hands. You also get a target to test things out.

### Rumble
Rumble is a pvp game that uses something that looks like Avatar the last Airbender’s earthbending. Allow the player to throw, summon and manipulate rocks based upon the position of their hands.

Rumble however uses controllers instead of hand-tracking, but since it does use the position of the player’s hands and their fingers on the controllers it is still a good game to test for this research.

There IS a way to play it with just hand-tracking[^1], but as you can see in the video, it isn’t optimal and moving is more difficult.

--- 

#### Gestures
Rumble uses position of hand and rotation of wrist more than it uses the position of fingers and the gestures you can make with your hand. Most gestures I got to test where two handed, some of them in a sequence, like holding your arms on your sides, and your lower arms pointing forward, your palms pointing upwards and you hands balled into fists, then punching your right hand forward and retreating your other hand to your chest to send out a disc-shaped rock.

--- 

#### Movement
Rumble uses controllers, and thus uses the joystick to move as your main way of movement. You can also move faster in a straight line by making a ‘jogging’ motion with your hands.

Later in the game you DO unlock a dash which allows you to move, but unfortunately, I didn’t unlock that move yet by the time I finished testing.

--- 

#### Tutorials
Rumble is a pvp game, so it gives you most tutorials in a testing ground. It also uses holographic hands to display what you should do. In the training grounds there is a screen that allows you to select a move and then the game will show you how to perform it, by using these holographic hands.

## Conclusion and Advice

### Gestures
Something to take away from Drakheir is the fact that spells are basically a ‘toggle’. Using one signature to ‘equip’ the spell and then another to get rid of it.

The ‘finishers’ from Drakheir could also be used as a way to represent the ‘final spell’ we are currently working on in secrets of Ignacios.

Once implemented, further research should be done in the form of user testing to see how it feels.

### Movement
The Drakheir tilt movement is not recommended to implement. It can easily be nauseating and disorienting. The fast movement it gives can be useful if you are used to it. But in a puzzle game where looking around is important and dodging isn’t as important, it is not recommended.

The hold and drag movement CAN work, but instead my advice is using the teleport, as it is easy to understand and can add to the magic aesthetic of Secrets of Ignacios.

### Tutorials
Both Drakheir and rumble use 3d holograms to guide the players into what they should do with their hands. The current concept for Drakheir is 2d drawings, so maybe we should look into what will work better. Or give the player to toggle between them.
