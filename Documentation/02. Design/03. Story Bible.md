# **Story Bible**

A world where there is no magic. Ignacio discovers that he can use excess energy in his body to perform magic. He wants to pass down his knowledge to someone like-minded and capable. He tested numerous people, but none of them were suited. When his own mortality grew closer, Ignacio realized that his knowledge would have to die with him, because he had yet to find a suitable successor. This prompted Ignacio to perform rituals on himself, thus increasing his lifespan, leading to his moniker: Ignacios the Undying.

He built a dungeon in order to secure his grimoire, waiting for someone to accept the challenge and succeed. The player has accepted the challenge to clear the dungeon after Ignacios the Undying noticed their potential. The player has a real knack for energy manipulation and figures out how the teleport spell works by experiencing it once and learning the hand gesture. The player gets teleported inside the dungeon via a teleportation circle.

The world is set 150 years after the fall of the great capital Valtoria. [^1]

- **The player**: has accepted the challenge of clearing the dungeon.
- **Ignacios the Undying**: master wizard wanting to find a successor.
- **The dungeon**: made in order to protect the grimoire of Ignacios the Undying.
- **The puzzles**: ways to test potential pupils and assert how they utilize certain spells.

[^1]: “Valtoria \- This name suggests power and grandeur, but also hints at decline ("Valtoria" could be short for "Vallatoria," meaning "falling tower")”
